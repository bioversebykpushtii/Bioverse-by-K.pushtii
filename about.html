🎓 About Me

I'm a Grade 12 PCB student with a deep curiosity for biology and a growing passion for technology. I love learning, creating, and challenging myself with new ideas.

🌿 Why Bioverse?

Bioverse began as a simple idea—to create a place where biology is easy to understand, visually engaging, and fun to learn. It's my personal project that combines my love for biology and creativity.

💻 My Journey

Alongside my studies, I'm learning HTML, CSS, Git, GitHub, and web development. Every page on Bioverse is built by me as I continue improving my skills.

🚀 My Vision

My dream is to transform Bioverse into a complete biology learning platform with notes, quizzes, projects, study trackers, and interactive tools for students.
