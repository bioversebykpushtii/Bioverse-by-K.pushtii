🌿 Who Am I?

I'm a Grade 12 PCB student with a deep curiosity for biology and a growing passion for technology. I created Bioverse to make biology more organized, engaging, and easier to understand—not just for myself, but for anyone who loves learning.

💻 My Journey

Alongside my studies, I'm learning web development and combining it with my love for biology. Every page, feature, and project on this website is something I'm building as I continue to learn and improve.

🚀 My Goal

My dream is to turn Bioverse into an educational platform where students can access notes, quizzes, projects, interactive study tools, and resources—all in one place.
